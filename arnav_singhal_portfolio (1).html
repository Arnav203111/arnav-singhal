A little about what I do and what drives me.

I’m currently pursuing a Bachelor’s in Journalism and Mass Communication (BJMC) at K.R. Mangalam University. I’m interested in the creative side of media — especially visual storytelling, photography, video production, social media content and graphic design. I like being involved in the complete process: from developing an idea and planning a shoot to editing the final piece and presenting it well.


I’m always looking to learn through practical projects, collaborate with people and build work that feels clear, engaging and thoughtfully made.
